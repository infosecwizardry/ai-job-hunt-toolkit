# Career interviewer prompt

Use this if you don't use Claude skills, or want a quick version that works in any AI chat tool. Attach your current resume, then paste the prompt. Change the role to the one you're targeting.

```
I'm targeting a SOC analyst role, and my current resume is attached.

Interview me about each job on it, one question at a time, until you've uncovered everything relevant. Ask what I did, why I did it, and what I did next. Ask about my routine work, unusual problems, things I improved, and anything that touches what a SOC analyst does, even a little. Look for security work I left off. Before moving to the next job, ask if there's anything else.

After each job, document what you learned as a list of work items. For each one, record what happened, what I did, why, what came next, the tools, my role (did it myself, led it, or assisted), and anything that shouldn't be claimed. Don't write resume bullets yet. This is my master list, and I'll write tailored bullets from it for each job listing later. If the roles I'm targeting require a security clearance, ask only its level and whether it's active or inactive. Use only facts I gave you, and ask if something is unclear.
```

Save everything it gives you in one document. That's your master list. The full skill in `skills/career-interviewer` also covers projects, training, and certifications, and hands you the finished master list as a file.
