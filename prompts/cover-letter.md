# Cover letter prompt

Use this if you don't use Claude skills, or want a quick version that works in any AI chat tool. Attach the job listing and your master list, then paste the prompt.

```
I'm applying for the attached job listing. My master list of my experience is also attached.

Before writing, ask me two questions, one at a time: whether I have a real reason for wanting this job, and whether there's anything I want them to know that doesn't fit on a resume.

Then write a cover letter in three or four short paragraphs, under one page. Open with the role and why I fit. Give one or two specific examples from my master list that answer the most important requirements in the listing. Show how I learn and work with others through something I actually do. Close with a plain ask for a conversation.

Write it the way I talk. Skip generic praise for the company and phrases like "I am writing to express my interest." Use only facts from my master list and my answers. If something is unclear, ask.
```
