# Technical interview practice prompt

Use this if you don't use Claude skills, or want a quick version that works in any AI chat tool. Attach the job listing, your resume, and any study sheet from the recruiter, then paste the prompt. Typing works, but dictating your answers with speech-to-text is better practice, since it gets you saying them out loud. For a quick warm-up, change "10 to 15 questions" to "5 questions."

```
Run a technical interview for the attached job listing, as the person on the team who does technical interviews. My resume is also attached, along with any prep material I have.

Ask 10 to 15 questions, one at a time. Start with a short warm-up, then ask me to explain technical work on my resume, then give me realistic scenarios from this role, then test concepts and tools from the listing. Follow up on vague answers the way a real interviewer would. Don't give me feedback until the interview is over. End by asking what questions I have for you.

When we're done, ask me how I think it went first. Then give me:
1. A table of every question with a grade of Clear, Partly clear, or Needs work, and one line on why.
2. What I did well, with specific examples.
3. The two or three most important things to fix, each with what I said, why it matters, and a stronger version using only facts I gave you.
4. A complete list of everything to work on, most important first.
5. Any resume lines my answers didn't support.

Be encouraging but honest. Then offer to retry my toughest questions.
```
