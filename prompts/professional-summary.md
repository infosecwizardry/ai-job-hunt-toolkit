# Professional summary prompt

Use this if you don't use Claude skills, or want a quick version that works in any AI chat tool. Attach the job listing and your resume, then paste the prompt.

```
I'm applying for the attached job listing. The resume I built for it is also attached, with a placeholder where the summary goes.

Before writing anything, ask me a few questions, one at a time, about what I'm doing to learn and grow outside of work and what I'm like on a team. Ask for specific examples, not adjectives.

Then write a 3 to 4 sentence summary that does three things: states the role I'm targeting, shows why my experience fits this listing, and shows who I'd be on the team through what I actually do. Avoid filler like "motivated," "passionate" or "detail-oriented." Write it the way a person talks, not like a template.

Give me two versions so I can pick or combine them. Use only facts from my resume and my answers. If something is unclear, ask.
```

The full skill in `skills/professional-summary` does more: it reads your resume first so it only asks about what's missing, includes worked examples, and shows you which sentence covers which part.
