# Projects section prompt

Use this if you don't use Claude skills, or want a quick version that works in any AI chat tool. Attach the job listing and your master list (or your resume), then paste the prompt.

```
I'm applying for the attached job listing. My master list is also attached. Help me write the Projects section of my resume.

Pick the two to four projects most relevant to this listing. Labs I designed and built myself count as projects; walkthroughs I followed step by step don't. For each one, even if my master list already describes it, ask me questions one at a time: what exact tools and data I used, what I set out to test, what broke and how I fixed it, what I did that wasn't in the tutorial, and what I plan to do next.

Then write each project as a title with its link (or a few words of context if there's no public link, never a made-up one), a one or two sentence summary, and two to four bullets aimed at this listing. Each bullet should name the exact tools, logs, and techniques, and show something I tested, found, or improved, not just a step I followed. No filler bullets. Use only facts from my master list and my answers, and ask if something is unclear.
```
